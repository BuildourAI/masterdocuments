# RatnKamal Enterprises AI Systems Blueprint

RatnKamal Enterprises swatahchya factory madhe GI metal che darwaje ani khidkya banavte ani te ghar bandhnarya vyaktinna ani builders na vikte, ₹10 – 50 Lakh mahinyachya band madhe. Tyanchya swatahchya mojnipramane pratyek mahinyala 200 leads yetat, 80 quotes jatat ani 35 orders book hotat, sarasari ₹45,000 chya, ani Production, Powder Coating, Glass Installation, Site Installation ani char lokanchya back office madhe 22 staff aahet. Custom software aadhich Order Form madhun Job Card Sheet banavte ani batch numbers ek department kadun dusrya department kade pudhe neto; enquiries, quotations, payment follow-ups ani malkancha sakalcha planning cha ek taas ajunahi haatane hotat. Ha document teen mahinyant paach jodlele layers suchavto, ani pratyek price, payment ani order madhla badal Santosh sign kartat.
