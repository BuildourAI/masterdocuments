00
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Overview

“Need to do sales and marketing setup for our company. CRM setup needs to do crm of my own and a follow-up system for customers, from generating leads to handing over the product to the customer retention process.” Shubham wrote that in his Systems Brief, and on the working call of 09.11.2026 he made it specific: the business is Kokan Nirvana Seashore property, a second-home brand inside Bhamchik Group, and what he wants is a real estate operating system that runs the whole sales process (paraphrase), with a tutorial for every property, “पूरा एटोमेटेड”.

Today 800 to 1000 leads a month arrive from Meta ads, the landing page and the portal, and every one of them is worked by hand: “विजिट के लिए हम मैन्योली फोन लगाते मैन्योली मैसेज करते मैन्योली रिसिट बेजते”. Ten to fifteen of them reach a quotation. There is no accounting system, no login for the 15 people, and the owner personally handles every deal, operation and audit.

This revision makes five structural changes, each traceable to the 09.11 call: (1) the brand and the goal are named, and every system now serves Kokan Nirvana; (2) a new system, the Property Tutorial Library, carries the tutorials he asked for; (3) the build is his own Real Estate OS in his own code and database, so the record becomes “crm of my own” and the current CRM runs beside it until the new one holds; (4) n8n becomes the automation layer for Meta leads, WhatsApp sends and schedules; (5) Rev A’s Login Desk and Day Open and Close Routine merge into one Team Desk for HR. The result is one foundation and eight systems in four phases over twelve weeks, with the lead flow live in weeks 1 to 3, inside his “before DEC” anchor.

It is built by Shubham himself, on his own Claude Max subscription, with several Claude Code sessions running side by side, one per system. Three companion documents ship with this revision: the Starter Guide (day 1, with Romana), the Continue Building Guide (every sitting after), and the Systems, Costs and Automation Guide (what each system costs to run, how the AI agents are built, and what n8n automates).

The call confirms Rev A’s seven stations and says plainly which ones hurt: “अभी हम लोग सब मैन्योली रे रहे”, then “विजिट के लिए हम मैन्योली फोन लगाते मैन्योली मैसेज करते मैन्योली रिसिट बेजते”. The call adds two facts. A landing page exists (“We have landing page.”), and Meta ads run at “month of $50,000 investment”. Shubham builds the OS himself in Claude Code, with Romana guiding the first setup live on a shared screen, and does not start before that session. Correction to the record: the call summary states the Max plan at “$10,000/month”. Claude Max is billed at about $100 a month (Max 5x) or $200 a month (Max 20x), roughly ₹10,000 or ₹20,000 with GST, so the summary’s figure reads as rupees, not dollars. Beyond Rev A’s list (ChatGPT, Base44, Cloud, Gemini), the call confirms a running Base44 subscription (“just have a subscription. base 44”) and no Claude subscription yet. Base44 is a no-code app builder; this build does not use it, so whether to keep paying is Shubham’s decision after Phase 1.

The call returns to the form’s central dashboard: lead management, HR and accounting in one place, every employee logging in to their own page (paraphrase), and on accounts, “Accounting is not happening.” Rev A already carried this across its finance and desk systems. BuildOur’s notes of the call record that Shubham wants to automate with n8n; the transcript does not carry the word legibly, so this rests on those notes. Rev B adopts it where it fits: n8n runs the pipes (Meta lead intake, WhatsApp sends, schedules, reminders), the app holds the record and the screens, and anything that calculates money stays in tested code. RECOMMENDED AI SYSTEMS
Foundation + 8 systems
KOKAN NIRVANA SINGLE RECORDF-00 · FOUNDATION
What it is

The Real Estate OS’s one database: every lead, property, unit, site visit, quotation, token, instalment, receipt and staff member, each stored once and linked. lead → property → visit → quotation → token → instalments → handover. The current CRM’s leads are imported once, and the CRM runs beside the OS until the new record holds.

Why first

“CRM setup needs to do crm of my own”. Every later system reads and writes this record; built once, it lets several systems be built at the same time without each keeping its own copy.

Open input

The CRM’s name, and an export of its leads (section 06).

KOKAN NIRVANA LEAD-TO-VISIT FLOWSYS-01 · FIRST DOMINO
What it does

A new lead from Meta lead ads, the landing page form, the portal or a walk-in becomes a record within minutes. The WhatsApp API sends the project details and the matching property tutorial in the Kokan Nirvana script, and a call task lands with the assigned staff member. The 5 to 6 follow-ups go out on set days and pause when the lead replies. When the lead says yes to a visit, slots are offered, booked, confirmed and reminded the day before. After the visit, the quotation is drafted with the price line blank and parked for the owner. lead → details + tutorial → call task → follow-ups → visit → quotation parked

Solves

§1.2: “मैन्योली फोन लगाते मैन्योली मैसेज करते”; Rev A station 02 to 04.

Guardrail

No price, discount, payment plan or token amount is ever set by the flow; the owner fills the price and taps approve. Messages use only templates the owner has signed.

Build

App screens and record in Next.js and Supabase; Meta intake, WhatsApp sends and the follow-up schedule in n8n.

KOKAN NIRVANA PROPERTY TUTORIAL LIBRARYSYS-02 · NEW IN REV B
What it does

One tutorial pack per property: a short walkthrough video (recorded on a phone, or produced with an AI video tool if Shubham chooses), location and distance from the city, unit or plot options, maintenance and rental-yield answers, and FAQs. AI drafts the script and the FAQ from the brochure and the Sales Skill; the owner approves each pack once. The Lead-to-Visit Flow sends the right pack automatically: on first contact, before a visit, and to a quiet lead. property → pack drafted → owner approves → sent by the flow → opened? logged

Solves

§1.3: “हर एक प्रॉपर्टी का टिटोरियल बनाऊंगा और पूरा एटोमेटेड रहेगा”.

Guardrail

No price, offer or payment plan appears in any pack; those stay in the quotation the owner fills.

Open input

The list of Kokan Nirvana properties and their material (section 06, step 03).

KOKAN NIRVANA SECOND HOME SALES SKILLSYS-03
What it does

The written sales rulebook the flows read: the questions for a new lead, the first WhatsApp reply, the 5 to 6 follow-ups spaced across the 2 to 3 month cycle, the visit pitch, the post-visit note, the handover checklist, the retention touches for the 10% who come back, and Kokan Nirvana’s answers to the second-home objections (maintenance, rental yield, distance from the city).

Solves

Rev A: each staff member writing their own WhatsApp messages from memory, with no fixed script from lead to handover.

Guardrail

Any price, offer or payment plan in a drafted message is typed in by the owner or sales manager before it goes out.

Adoption

Two or three hours of the sales manager dictating in weeks 1 to 2. It is a writing lane: it runs in its own session beside the code from day 1.

KOKAN NIRVANA RECEIPT AND LEDGER APPSYS-04
What it does

When money lands in the Bhamchik bank account and the owner or sales manager ticks it, the receipt is entered once: customer, unit, amount, mode, date. A numbered receipt PDF goes out on WhatsApp, the credit is posted to the ledger and the unit’s outstanding updates. Salary, incentive, brokerage, site and office expenses are entered with a photo of the bill. money lands → tick → entered once → receipt sent → ledger + outstanding

Solves

§1.2 “मैन्योली रिसिट बेजते”; §1.7 “Accounting is not happening.”; the form’s “No accounting system in our company”.

Guardrail

A receipt is generated only after a person ticks that the money has actually landed. Receipt numbers and totals are calculated by tested code, never by AI.

KOKAN NIRVANA PAYMENT REMINDER FLOWSYS-05
What it does

Runs off each unit’s instalment schedule in the ledger. Before a due date it checks nothing has been received, sends the reminder with name, unit, amount and date on WhatsApp, sends a second reminder if the date passes and flags the unit to the sales manager and owner, and stops the moment a receipt is generated.

Solves

Rev A station 07: instalment follow-up by whoever remembers.

Guardrail

Any extension, waiver, late charge or change to an amount is never sent by the flow; the owner writes it.

Open input

The standard instalment schedule for a sale (section 06, step 06).

KOKAN NIRVANA FUNNEL BOARDSYS-06
What it does

One screen for the owner and sales manager: leads by Meta campaign, landing page and portal; calls made, tutorials sent and opened, visits booked, quotations out and their age; overdue follow-ups by staff name; handovers and retention touches due; cost per lead and per visit by campaign, read from the Meta ad account.

Solves

§1.4 and Rev A: no view of which campaign brings buyers who visit; the owner checking every deal by phone.

Guardrail

Any change to Meta ad spend is made by the owner, never by the board.

KOKAN NIRVANA FINANCE BOARDSYS-07
What it does

Built on the ledger: cash in bank, money due this week and month unit by unit, salary and incentive owed, brokerage and site bills, a running profit and loss for the month, and a red mark wherever the ledger and the bank disagree.

Solves

The form’s “all credit debit entry / profit & loss” and the owner auditing every deal personally.

Guardrail

Nothing moves from the board. Any payment, release of incentive or change to a schedule happens only when the owner marks it approved.

Open input

The incentive rules, written and signed once before any incentive figure is drafted.

Merges Rev A’s Login Desk and Day Open and Close Routine.

Solves

The form’s “one central dashboard for my company... employee login to dashboard user ID and password” and §1.7.

Guardrail

Every new login, role change and access to finance pages waits for the owner’s approval. No staff page shows a money figure.
