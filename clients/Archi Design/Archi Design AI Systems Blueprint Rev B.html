00
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Overview

“My Business is Not growing because I am involved in daily office activities rather than focusing on a growth. Employees don’t take ownership. I have to follow up constantly.” Mahesh wrote that in his Systems Brief, and the Lean Master Plan v1.4 now on file turns it into one principle: manage by exception. The team works from a simple daily list, the system chases, escalates and scores, and the owner looks at one screen that shows only what needs him.

Archi Design wins work: 2 orders a month at an average of ₹10,00,000, 60% from returning clients, inside a 15-day sales cycle. What it cannot do is run that work without the owner. Every quote, price and follow-up is his, tasks are given by voice and WhatsApp, drawings reach site as WhatsApp images, site engineers who go straight to site are missed by the eSSL machine, and there is no standard format to measure anyone’s work.

This revision makes six structural changes, each traceable to the master plan: (1) Attendance and Leave becomes a Phase 1 system, with the eSSL machine kept and a phone punch for site days; (2) the Daily Task Sheet becomes My Day with priority numbers, and the Nudge chain merges into it; (3) organisation, departments and approval limits enter the foundation; (4) Leads and Proposals move from month 10 into Phase 2; (5) Turnkey Controls by project type and size are added; (6) the AI helpers (Nandini, the Site Controller and the Digital Ops Manager) are parked until the basics work, because the master plan excludes AI assistants from the first release. The result is one foundation and nine systems in four phases over twelve months, with Phase 1 in six weeks.

It is built by Mahesh’s team on his own Claude Max in Claude Code, on Next.js, Supabase and Vercel, with all code, data and accounts in the studio’s name, which meets the master plan’s full-ownership requirement without an outside vendor.

01
WHAT CHANGED SINCE REV A
Delta record

Rev A’s current state (the funnel, the owner as the only link, the three problems in Mahesh’s words) remains valid and is carried in panels 01 to 05. This section records only what the Lean Master Plan v1.4 adds or changes.

1.1A master plan is now on file

The Lean Business Management Software Master Plan v1.4 (November 2026) sets out nine modules, about 14 screens, five lean tests every feature must pass, a twelve-month roadmap and success targets at 12 months. Its cover still carries “[Studio Name]”; this blueprint treats it as Archi Design’s plan, which Mahesh should confirm. It governs where it is newer and more specific than the brief.

1.2Attendance: the eSSL machine stays, site days count

The design and site teams use the eSSL machine, but site engineers who go straight to site, or home from site, are not recorded. The plan’s answer: one record a day from three sources (machine, phone punch, correction request), location captured only at the moment of punching, and the studio’s current rule built in: 10 minutes grace, three late marks in a month lead to a deduction of half a day’s salary.

PainA site engineer at site by 9 AM is marked absent because the machine is in the office; late marks and leave are counted by hand from registers.

1.3Turnkey projects of five types

Rev A described an interior and architecture practice. The plan adds turnkey work across interior, renovation, civil, commercial and factory projects, where cost overruns, contractor delays, safety and cash flow move to the studio. It answers with one engine, five templates, three size tiers and nine turnkey controls switched on by type and size, and a weekly one-page Project Pulse for larger projects.

PainBudgets, contractor bills and purchases are tracked by the owner; cost overruns are seen late (Rev A, accounts row).

1.4Organisation, departments and authority

Rev A recorded managers and reporting lines as “not answered”. The plan defines up to five levels, six departments (Business and Client Relations; Architecture and Design; Projects and Site; Estimation, Procurement and Contracts; Accounts; HR and Admin), two reporting lines that never conflict (line manager for leave and appraisal, project captain for tasks), an approval matrix with value bands, and who sees what. Every limit is in brackets: they are Mahesh’s to set.

1.5Every task carries a priority number

Tasks reach a person from the owner, captains, leads and colleagues, and the person guesses what comes first. The plan’s rule: one ordered list per person, numbered 1 to 5, levels Do first, Today, This week and Later, only leaders may set Do first and one at a time, a clash button so the employee never referees, and auto ranking for template tasks. This sharpens Rev A’s Daily Task Sheet into My Day.

1.6A studio-level layer: Scaling Up

The plan adds the Scaling Up rhythm (Verne Harnish): a one-page Studio Plan, 3 to 5 quarterly Rocks, one Critical Number, a Digital Huddle each morning in place of a standing huddle, a generated Weekly Review agenda, a function accountability chart, and a studio cash view. It is one screen and a few routines, not a heavy module.

1.7Leads move up; client satisfaction gets a target

Rev A placed the CRM for Instagram, Just Dial and referral in months 10 to 12. The plan puts Leads and Proposals in its first build, as a growth lever: every enquiry tracked with a next action and a first response inside 30 minutes. It also sets a 95%+ client satisfaction target with three one-tap rating points, a call within 24 hours for any score below 8, and a Green, Amber or Red health colour per project.

1.8What is deliberately left out

The plan excludes, until the basics work: Gantt charts, complex Kanban boards, public leaderboards, 360-degree appraisals, AI assistants, a vendor portal, full payroll processing, continuous GPS tracking, referral engines and festival-greeting automation. Rev A’s three AI employees (Nandini, the Site Controller, the Digital Ops Manager) therefore become D-01: the morning list, reminders and escalation run as rules in My Day, and the AI helpers return in the plan’s Phase 4 as optional.

1.9The build path: self-build, mapped to the plan’s requirements

The plan was written for an outside vendor (React or Next.js, PostgreSQL, Flutter or React Native, AWS Mumbai, an RFP, sprint demos). Rev B keeps the owner’s self-build on Claude Max and maps the requirements onto it: Next.js for the app, PostgreSQL through Supabase in the Mumbai region, Vercel hosting, all code in the studio’s GitHub, rules in Settings, offline site entries, and a phone web app added to the home screen instead of store apps at first. The eSSL link is confirmed in week 1 (model and export method), with a scheduled file import as the fallback. If Mahesh prefers a vendor, this blueprint serves as the scope.

02
RECOMMENDED AI SYSTEMS
Foundation + 9 systems
ARCHI DESIGN STUDIO RECORD AND ORGANISATIONF-00 · FOUNDATION
What it is

One database for every project, stage, person, task, drawing, punch and approval, plus the organisation: departments, levels, line manager and project captain for each person, deputies, and the approval matrix with value bands. Settings let the admin add a branch, team, service line or entity without a developer. person → department → line manager · project → captain → tasks

Why first

Every later system reads it, and the approval limits decide who signs what from day one. It is also the master plan’s growth headroom: the same record serves a studio many times larger by settings, not code.

Open input

The org chart as it is today and every value band (section 06).

ARCHI DESIGN STUDIO WAYS: SOPS, TEMPLATES, STUDIO PLANSYS-01
What it does

The writing lane, run from day 1. Holds the workflow for every stage (brief, concept, design development, working drawings, BOQ, execution, handover, after handover) with a definition of done and the proof each task needs; five project templates (interior, renovation, civil, commercial, factory) and three size tiers; quality gates (waterproofing, electrical and plumbing, false ceiling, carpentry, painting and flooring, final finishing); role cards with four metrics; and the one-page Studio Plan.

Solves

Rev A: “Employees dont follow SOP’s”, and SOPs that “live in your head”.

Adoption

Two weeks of sittings with the owner; leads co-design the scorecards so the rules feel fair (master plan §17).

ARCHI DESIGN MY DAY AND ESCALATIONSYS-02 · FIRST DOMINO
What it does

Each person opens one list of 3 to 5 tasks numbered in the order to do them, with the reason for each number. Givers choose a level; the system sets the number and shows the effect before sending. Done needs proof (file, photo or note); Blocked and Clash alert the captain at once. People set their own dates each Monday; an evening two-tap check-in closes the day. A missed deadline triggers a reminder, then the captain, then the department head, then the owner. task + level → number → done with proof → reminder → ladder

Solves

“Everything gets completed because I have take personally follow up each and every day by giving them daily tasks and taking continuously follow up.”

Guardrail

The system never shortens or extends a deadline or changes an owner; a push by a leader is tagged “priority change” and never counts against the person. At most 3 notifications a day per person.

ARCHI DESIGN ATTENDANCE AND LEAVESYS-03 · NEW IN REV B
What it does

Builds one attendance record a day from the eSSL machine, a one-tap phone punch (server time, location and accuracy, site zone check) and correction requests. Applies 10 minutes grace and the three-late-marks rule, shows each person “Late marks this month: 1 of 3”, handles site shift, on-duty, comp-off and leave in three taps, and exports a month-end summary to the payroll tool, Tally or Excel.

Solves

Master plan §9: site staff “cannot punch at the office machine”; HR “handled in registers and messages”.

Guardrail

Location only at punch, never between punches, after punch-out or on leave; visible to the person, their manager, HR and the owner only; kept for a set period. Waivers and outside-zone punches need the manager with a logged reason. The deduction rule must be in letters and policies staff have acknowledged; confirm with an HR or legal adviser.

Open input

eSSL model, firmware and export method; employee machine IDs; shifts; site addresses for zones.

ARCHI DESIGN LEADS AND PROPOSALSSYS-04 · MOVED UP
What it does

Every enquiry from Instagram, Just Dial, referral or the website lands in one five-stage pipeline (New, Contacted, Site Visit, Proposal Sent, Won or Lost) with an owner and a next-action date; an amber alert after 24 hours without action. Proposals come from templates with fee stages and a tracked link. Marking a lead Won creates the project from its template and puts the first week’s tasks on My Day.

Solves

Rev A: “Every quote, every price and every follow up is yours, and nothing is recorded beyond your phone.”

Guardrail

Any fee below standard or discount waits for the owner (or the Business Head within a set percentage) and is logged with the reason.

ARCHI DESIGN DRAWING AND SITE CHAINSYS-05
What it does

A drawing register with status (Draft, Internal Review, For Client Approval, Released for Site, Superseded) and revision history; the site task stays locked until release. The site diary (photos first, works offline) records each day, measurement checks and readiness checks; a site question becomes a task for the designer with a deadline; quality gates need a photo checklist; snags and change orders are tracked; a design slip shifts dependent site tasks and alerts the people affected; a Friday three-week look-ahead lists drawings, materials and client decisions due.

Solves

“Tracking pending drawings, approvals and site requirements. Knowing the actual status of every project without calling someone.”

Guardrail

Every drawing is reviewed by the design lead, signed by the client and released by the captain or owner before it reaches site; nothing goes to a contractor without it.

ARCHI DESIGN OWNER EXCEPTION VIEW AND STUDIO PLANSYS-06
What it does

One screen for the owner: red projects with the reason, tasks blocked over 24 hours, approvals waiting, low client scores, people missing commitments, revenue and pipeline against target, this quarter’s Rocks and the Critical Number; “All clear” when nothing needs him. Team board for captains (queues, drag to reorder, clashes, load) and a department head view. A Digital Huddle each morning and a generated Weekly Review agenda.

Solves

Rev A: walking the office, calling the site, and still not knowing the real status.

ARCHI DESIGN SCORECARDS AND INCENTIVESSYS-07
What it does

Four metrics per role, calculated from records already in the system (for example a designer: on-time stages, first-time-right approvals, client satisfaction on design, promises kept). Two months in shadow mode, then a project incentive pool, a share of each project’s gross margin set by the owner, split by role and score band, paid only if the project’s client satisfaction and margin are on plan. A 12-month history supports appraisal.

Solves

“I cannot able to measure my employee’s work in standard format.”

Guardrail

The owner signs every monthly scorecard before any incentive, deduction or appraisal figure is shown. Client, authority and supplier delays are excluded visibly; no public ranking; attendance is never counted twice; every manual adjustment logged with a reason.

ARCHI DESIGN CLIENT PAGE AND PULSESYS-08
What it does

Each client gets a page with progress, photos, the next decision needed, documents and one-tap approvals. Three one-tap ratings (after design approval, mid-execution, at handover) and a recommend score at handover. Any score below 8 asks the captain to call within 24 hours; 6 or below alerts the owner. Each project shows Green, Amber or Red.

Solves

“So clients satisfaction index is becoming lower.”

Guardrail

The system never promises a date, scope or cost to a client; any reply to an unhappy client is drafted and approved by the owner.

ARCHI DESIGN TURNKEY CONTROLS, BY TYPE AND TIERSYS-09 · NEW IN REV B
What it does

Switched on only for projects whose type and tier need them: contract and scope baseline, budget by cost code with committed and actual cost, procurement with quote comparison and long-lead tracking, contractor work orders and running bills, site store with challan photos, progress measured by BOQ value, client billing and project cash flow, safety and statutory trackers, and a weekly one-page Project Pulse. Later, the studio cash view (days to invoice, days to collect, cash conversion cycle).

Solves

Master plan §4: turnkey risk on cost, contractors, safety and cash; Rev A: “Cost overruns are seen late.”

Guardrail

Purchase orders, change orders and vendor payments are approved by value band; money never moves from the system; accounting and statutory work stay in the accounting tool.

Note

If a large civil, commercial or factory project is already running, budget, work orders and site store come forward for that project.

Note: the existing stack

Instagram, Just Dial, referral: kept as lead sources, entering SYS-04. eSSL machine: kept, linked to SYS-03; never replaced. Payroll tool, Tally or Excel: kept; receives the attendance and incentive export; full payroll is not built. WhatsApp: stays for conversation; system messages start inside the app and by email, and move to the WhatsApp Business API only with the owner’s yes on its cost. Added: Claude Max, GitHub, Supabase, Vercel.

Deferred · parked, not dropped

D-01 AI helpers: Nandini, Site Controller, Digital Ops Manager. The master plan leaves AI assistants out until the basics work; the same morning list, reminders and escalation run as rules in SYS-02 and SYS-06. Re-entry: months 11 to 12, as optional helpers for status answers and proposal drafts, on the Claude API with the owner’s yes on cost. D-02 Forecasts: 13-week cash forecast, margin by client and project type, price what-if panel; months 11 to 12.
