Do minute

Aapne likha tha: “My Business is Not growing because I am involved in daily office activities rather than focusing on a growth. Employees don’t take ownership. I have to follow up constantly.” Blueprint Rev B (jisme aapka Lean Master Plan bhi shaamil hai) iska jawab ek principle se deta hai: manage by exception. Team ek simple daily list se kaam karti hai, system follow-up, escalation aur scoring karta hai, aur aap sirf ek screen dekhte ho jisme wahi hai jo aapko chahiye. Phase 1 (6 hafte) mein teen cheezein banti hain: Studio Ways (aapke SOPs aur templates), My Day (har insaan ki 1 se 5 numbered task list) aur Attendance (eSSL machine ke saath phone punch, site wale dino ke liye).

Coding aani zaroori nahi. Har line Claude Code likhega. Aapka kaam: uske sawalon ka jawab apne shabdon mein do, sahi lage to OK bolo, aur jo bana hai use apne jaane-pehchane projects aur logon se check karo. Kuch samajh na aaye to likho ye mujhe simple shabdon mein samjhao. Kuch toot jaaye to error ya screenshot paste karke likho ye simple shabdon mein samjhao aur theek karo. Permission maange aur samajh na aaye to pehle likho ye kya karega, simple shabdon mein batao?

THE RULES: FOR CLAUDE.md, WORD FOR WORD
Stage 2 mein Claude Code padhega · English mein
Standing rules for everything ever built in this folder, in every session

House rules. 1 · ONE HOME: this folder, repository, database and app are the only ones Archi Design will ever have; every system is a new section of the same app reading the same projects, people and logins; a branch, team, service line or entity is added in Settings, never as a new app. 2 · EVERY PERSON THEIR OWN KEY: every person has their own login and sees only what their role needs (Blueprint Rev B, section 02 note on visibility); the owner sees everything, with every override logged; no shared passwords. 3 · A HUMAN YES BEFORE ANYTHING LEAVES THE OFFICE OR MOVES MONEY: no message about dates, extra work or cost goes to a client, contractor or vendor, and no change order, purchase order, work order, discount or payment is approved, without the approver set in the approval matrix; one switch turns all automatic behaviour off without code. 4 · THE DIARY NOBODY CAN EDIT: every action is written to an append-only audit log. 5 · SECRETS STAY IN THE CUPBOARD: keys live only in .env.local and Vercel’s settings, never in code, chat or GitHub. 6 · NUMBERS ARE CALCULATED, NEVER GUESSED: deadlines, priority numbers, attendance status, late marks, scores, budgets and incentives come from exact tested rules; AI never invents a figure. If anything you propose bends a rule, say so and wait for my decision.

Archi Design rules (from Blueprint Rev B, until Mahesh writes his own guardrails). AI may act alone only to: remind a person of their own task and its proof; rank tasks by the auto ranking rules; check a drawing’s name and sheet list against the SOP; import punches and apply the attendance rules; compile summaries, scoreboards and scorecards for the owner. A person signs off: pricing and negotiation; every drawing before Released for Site; change orders, purchase and work orders, discounts and vendor payments by value band; every message to a client, contractor or vendor about dates, extra work or cost; every monthly scorecard before any incentive, deduction or appraisal figure is shown; late-mark waivers, corrections and outside-zone punches. Never automated: any quote, price, discount or committed date; design judgement and client advice; hiring, appraisal outcomes and consequences; continuous location tracking; public ranking of people. Location is captured only at punch in, punch out or site check-in, visible only to the person, their line manager, HR and the owner. Every task has one owner and a priority number; only leaders may set Do first, one at a time per person; a push by a leader is tagged “priority change” and never counts against the person; the system never shortens or extends a deadline. At most 3 notifications per person per day. Scorecards have at most four metrics per role, run two months in shadow mode, exclude client, authority and supplier delays visibly, and never count attendance twice; values are discussed, never scored. An approval waiting over 1 working day moves up one level; a named deputy covers leave. Rules, templates, gates, weights, thresholds and attendance settings live in Settings, editable by the admin, never in code. No form has more than 5 required fields; staff screens never show reports; labels use the studio’s own words. AI helpers stay out until months 11 to 12 and only with Mahesh’s yes. Unconfirmed blueprint figures are never hard-coded; docs/samples never go to GitHub.

THE BUILD BRIEF: FOR CLAUDE CODE
Mahesh ji ye page skip kar sakte hain

Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind) with theme tokens (Light, Dark, three palettes and the studio brand, chosen per user), Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Row Level Security, Storage) in the Mumbai region through @supabase/ssr and @supabase/supabase-js, deployed on Vercel from main; branches get preview deployments. Mobile-first web app added to the home screen; site diary and phone punch work offline and sync, using server time. Phone punch uses the browser’s location only at the moment of punching, checked against site zones. eSSL link: confirm the model in week 1; preferred automatic sync, fallback a scheduled file import, matched by employee machine ID. Scheduled jobs (reminders, escalation, Digital Huddle, month-end) as Vercel Cron calling protected routes. Notifications in-app and email first; WhatsApp only with Mahesh’s yes. Variables: NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL, NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY, SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY, later CRON_SECRET; .env.example with names only. Every screen follows the screens, look and visibility note in Blueprint Rev B section 02.

F-00 Studio Record and Organisation: the starting shape

departments; positions (level L0 to L5, department); profiles (name, phone, role, position, department, line manager, deputy, theme, language, machine ID, shift, normal place of work, active); approval_limits (decision type, role, value band, deputy, escalation hours); projects (client, type interior / renovation / civil / commercial / factory, tier 1 to 3, captain, stage, start and end dates, health colour); project_members; templates, template_stages, template_tasks; tasks (project, owner, why it matters, committed date, status, blocked reason, depends on, priority level, priority number, set by, reason for rank, pushed by); rules (type, values, changed by, changed on); settings (automation switch off by default); audit_log (append only). Row Level Security as in the visibility note of Blueprint Rev B section 02. Attendance, drawings, site updates, leads, scores, budgets and the Studio Plan come in their own systems’ migrations.

Sessions

  • MAIN owns main, CLAUDE.md, docs/SESSIONS.md and the database; applies migrations in order and merges finished work.
  • One builder session per system, at most three at a time, each in its own git worktree on branch sys-0N-short-name, touching only app/(sys-0N)/ and lib/sys-0N/; new tables only in a new migration <timestamp>_sys0N_<what>.sql, adding, never dropping. Shared files change only in MAIN; requests in docs/requests/, handovers in docs/handovers/.
  • Writing lane (SYS-01) writes only in docs/ and content/ from day 1.
  • Talk to Mahesh in simple Hinglish; build the system in English. Plan first, wait for OK; every screen follows docs/design and the UI Guide; a dated note in docs/sessions/ each sitting.
